# Editorial role corrections after verified player-fact corrections

The source-backed field pipeline accepts a narrow, typed role correction on an
already-adjudicated player-facts parent. This closes the composition gap where
correcting a player's birthday prevents the older raw-row identity guard from
accepting an otherwise evidenced role correction. The older guard is unchanged.

The adapter replays the original official-facts plan and requires its complete
recipe, JUG and EQ postimages to equal the registered parent. It then binds one
source identity, exclusive native owner, original annual row and either a
start-year fixture witness or independently retained dated lineup and midfield
report. The separate dated-lineup policy admits August–December evidence in the
season start year. It is currently limited to a sourced broad midfielder with
incompatible inherited factory roles. Central midfield is an explicit editorial
compatible default, not a claimed published fine position.

Only that player's six role slots may change; other native bytes and players
remain identical. The recipe gains the fine role, a source-plan artifact and a
reviewed-editorial proof. It removes the corresponding preserve-native fallback.
The common publisher and SOURCE reconstruction retain their existing paths.

Source hashes are semantic plan inputs. Verification code hashes invalidate
in-process replay caches and are captured separately in QA receipts; they must
not change a recipe's transaction identity merely because verification code
changes. Every nested retained source binding is rehashed before cache lookup.

Validation: `scripts/test_season_dated_parent_facts_editorial_role_v1.py` covers
a second synthetic person/year, wrong identity/date/side, changed source bodies,
actual cache hits and invalidation, and plan equality across verification-code
fingerprint changes. Retained local parent checks are additional corpus evidence
and skip when those local artifacts are unavailable. Native and full SOURCE
readback plus runner evidence are still required before selecting a release.

The null-annual-DOB branch also supports a literal source goalkeeper role,
mapped to native role0. It requires the verified corrected-facts parent, exact
annual-row identity, period source context and exclusive native owner. The
existing copied-factory-role guard and exact six-role-byte mask remain active.
The DOB/country step and role step are separate deterministic layers; neither
changes height, ratings, ownership, tactics or artwork. The original defender
and midfielder plans must continue to replay byte-for-byte.
